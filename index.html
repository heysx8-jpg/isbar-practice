Live generator: a new case every time

Finished the case bank? Generate a fresh case for any condition, age, gender and setting, then a sample 7-minute answer with coaching notes.

How to practise with a partner

  1. Prepare for 10 minutes. Open or print a case and plan on its ISBAR planning sheet. Don't look at the sample answer.
  2. Present for 7 minutes. Your partner plays your clinical supervisor and times you (each case page has a timer).
  3. Questions for 3 minutes. The “supervisor” asks follow-up questions. The coaching notes in each sample answer suggest some.
  4. Compare. Read the sample answer. Did you spot the immediate priority? Did any distractors pull you off course? Was most of your time spent on Assessment and Recommendation?

Each case is 540–600 words and hides one immediate priority among realistic distractors. Sample answers are about 950–1,050 spoken words (about 7 minutes). They are one reasonable response, not the only correct one.
